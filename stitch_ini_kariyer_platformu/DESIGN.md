## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Core Purpose
The platform connects undergraduate university students (specifically starting with computer science and electrical engineering majors in Turkey) with verified corporate internships and academic research lab positions. The brand core is grounded in **rigorous authenticity, academic discipline, and local craftsmanship**. 

Unlike conventional hiring platforms dominated by corporate navy blues or synthetic purples, this design system draws inspiration from historic Anatolian ceramic craft ("Çini")—specifically Iznik tilework and Seljuk turquoise glazes. It pairs deep, mineral turquoise and crisp crystalline white porcelain foundations with measured, functional coral red ("Mercan") and verification emeralds.

### Emotional Response
- **Grounded Trust & Verification:** Students feel reassured that every listing, requirement, and lab contact is backed by an auditable citation date and verified source URL.
- **Clarity & Scholarly Freshness:** Visual tone feels energetic, intellectual, and unbloated—respecting the student’s focus without bureaucratic fatigue.
- **Urgency without Panic:** Critical application deadlines stand out decisively through precise, functional Coral indicators without visual clutter.

### Design Movement: Modern Academic Minimalism with Ceramic Tactility
A crisp, high-clarity structural layout combining:
- Clean, open whitespace and structured data tables reminiscent of peer-reviewed documentation.
- Subtle ceramic glaze surface tones (`#F0FDFA` / `#F8FAFC`) with soft, mineral-tinted borders.
- Explicit visual badges differentiating Corporate vs. Academic Research labs, source verification levels, and AI match compatibility scores.

## Colors

### Color Strategy & Philosophy
The palette avoids standard enterprise tech conventions by using the rich tonal spectrum of Anatolian Çini ceramics:
- **Primary (`#087E8B` - İznik Turkuazı):** Represents verified truth, high-signal intelligence, and institutional credibility. Applied across key interactive controls, dominant state highlights, and navigation.
- **Secondary (`#0E7490` - Derin Deniz / Deep Cyan):** Anchors secondary navigational cues, research metadata accents, and academic hierarchy tags.
- **Tertiary (`#00A896` - Mineral Yeşil-Mavi):** Reserved for positive matching scores, progress meters, and dynamic profile compatibility.
- **Neutral (`#1E293B` - Slate Taş):** High-contrast, warm dark slate for typographic clarity, avoiding pure uncalibrated black to preserve reading comfort across dense specs and citation extracts.

### Strict Functional Color Rules
- **Mercan / Coral Accent (`#E05A47`):** Strictly dedicated to time-critical status indicators (e.g., "Son 3 Gün", closing deadlines within 72 hours, critical expiration warnings). It must never be applied to general buttons, brand embellishments, or decoration.
- **Doğrulama (Verification) Emerald (`#059669`):** Indicates verified official source links (YÖK Akademik, verified lab pages, active career portals).
- **İnceleme (Under Review / Unknown) Amber (`#D97706`):** Explicitly alerts students when a lab acceptance status or requirement is unconfirmed ("Bilinmiyor" or unverified scraping).
- **Akademik Lab Indicator (`#4338CA`):** Distinguishes faculty research and unpaid/grant laboratory investigations from private sector corporate internships.

## Typography

### Typographic Rationale
**Plus Jakarta Sans** serves as the universal type system across display, body, and labels. Its geometric foundations evoke clean, contemporary engineering clarity, while its humanist terminal counters prevent visual dryness in academic reading.

### Hierarchy & Usage
- **Display & Headings:** Tightly tracked headings (`-0.025em` to `-0.01em`) preserve compactness in technical titles (e.g., "Doğal Dil İşleme & LLM Araştırma Stajyeri").
- **Body & Requirement Excerpts:** Set with deliberate line-height multipliers (`1.55` to `1.6`) to provide comfortable scanning of qualifications, professor publications, and condition requirements.
- **Metadata, Badges & Proof Citations:** Use `label-sm` in tabular uppercase or capitalized formats with `0.04em` letter spacing for source checks (e.g., `KAYNAK: TEYİT EDİLDİ (12 EKİM)`).

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Architecture
The platform utilizes a structured 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to display multi-attribute candidate listings side-by-side with match reasoning and source citations.
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column layout with `gutter: 1.5rem` and `margin: 2rem`. Typical workspace splits into a 4-column filter/profile sidebar and an 8-column feed or dual-pane card viewer (Listing details vs. Eligibility Analysis).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `gutter: 1rem` and `margin: 1.5rem`. Filter bar shifts to a collapsable drawer or sticky horizontal pill filter strip.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `gutter-sm: 1rem` and `margin-sm: 1rem`. Opportunity cards occupy 4 full columns with vertical metadata stacking.

### Spacing Cadence
Spacing follows an exact 4px/8px modular rhythm:
- `space-xs` (4px) for micro badge gaps, tag icon separations, and inline metric tags.
- `space-sm` (8px) for card sub-item groupings, requirement checklist items, and input padding.
- `space-md` (16px) for interior card content padding and related form controls.
- `space-lg` (24px) for card-to-card vertical gaps, panel headers, and modal padding.
- `space-xl` (40px) for layout section divisions and hero/dashboard category breaks.

## Elevation & Depth

### Philosophy: Mineral Planes & Glaze Borders
Rather than standard deep drop shadows, this design system models depth on fine ceramic tiles: smooth, clean white porcelain surfaces set over subtle cool-grey and glazed turquoise grounds. Depth is communicated primarily through crisp hairline boundaries, tinted overlays, and diffuse ambient contact shadows.

### Elevation Scales
- **Ground / Base (`Level 0`):** `#F8FAFC` canvas foundation with zero shadow.
- **Card / Surface Default (`Level 1`):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border, cast with a microscopic mineral shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Card Interactive / Hover (`Level 2`):** Elevates via a turquoise tinted rim and slight lift: border shifts to `#CCFBF1`, shadow expands to `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(8, 126, 139, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Active Modals & Critical Flyouts (`Level 3`):** `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` combined with a frosted backdrop (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background: rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)`).

## Shapes

### Form Language: Balanced Structural Softness (`roundedness: 2`)
- **Containers & Cards:** Styled with `0.5rem` (8px) base radius, stepping up to `1rem` (16px) for major modal viewports and feed cards (`rounded-lg`).
- **Interactive Controls:** Buttons, input fields, and search boxes adhere strictly to `0.5rem` (8px), maintaining an architectural, dependable feel suited for academic tooling.
- **Status Tags, Verification Badges & Match Scores:** Rendered with full pill styling (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to distinctly contrast functional data badges against rectangular card surfaces.

## Components

### Opportunity Cards (Fırsat Kartları)
Opportunity cards are the central entity of the interface, designed to distinguish between corporate internships and academic research groups:
- **Card Structure:** White `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E2E8F0` stroke, 16px radius, `space-lg` inner padding.
- **Category Badge:** 
  - *Academic Lab:* `#EEF2FF` fill, `#4338CA` text, and `#C7D2FE` border with an institutional building or flask glyph.
  - *Corporate Internship:* `#F0FDFA` fill, `#087E8B` text, and `#CCFBF1` border with a briefcase glyph.
- **Match Score Indicator (Eşleşme Rozeti):** High-prominence pill positioned in top-right corner. Features a circular SVG progress track tinted in `#00A896` with numerical percentage (e.g., `%94 Eşleşme`).
- **Source Verification Timestamp:** Footnote metadata row displaying: Source domain link, verification date, and verification status dot (Emerald `#059669` for verified, Amber `#D97706` for unconfirmed / "Bilinmiyor").
- **Coral Urgency Banner ("Son Başvuru"):** When deadline is <= 3 days, a dedicated `#FEF2F2` banner with `#E05A47` bold label and alert icon appears at the card top edge.

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Action (e.g., "E-posta Taslağı Oluştur", "Uygunluğu İncele"):** Solid `#087E8B` background, `#FFFFFF` text, 8px border radius, medium font weight (`600`). On hover: `#0E7490`.
- **Secondary Action (e.g., "Kaynağa Git", "Takibe Al"):** Background `#F0FDFA`, border 1px solid `#CCFBF1`, text `#087E8B`. On hover: `#E0F2FE`.
- **Urgent Action / Danger:** Never used for generic actions. Reserved exclusively for application deadline confirmations with `#E05A47` border and text.

### Verification & Match Badges (Rozetler)
- **Doğrulanmış Kaynak (Verified):** Pill container in `#ECFDF5` with `#059669` text, accompanied by an unbroken checkmark icon and text: `Doğrulandı: [Tarih]`.
- **Teyit Bekliyor / Bilinmiyor (Unconfirmed / Unknown):** Pill container in `#FFFBEB` with `#D97706` text and exclamation glyph: `Kabul Durumu: Bilinmiyor`.
- **Eksik Şart (Requirement Gap):** Small pill in `#FEF2F2` with `#E05A47` text indicating missing candidate prerequisites extracted from user profile.

### Input Fields & Search Filters
- **Text & Link Inputs:** Clean white `#FFFFFF` surface with 1px `#CBD5E1` border, 8px radius, `0.75rem 1rem` padding.
- **Active / Focus State:** Crisp 2px halo in `#087E8B` at 20% opacity with a solid `#087E8B` inner border. Never rely solely on generic blue browser outlines.
- **Direct Link Importer ("İlan Linki Yapıştır"):** Specialized input featuring an embedded action button on the right edge and clear microcopy assuring instant source and requirement extraction.

### Application Tracker (Başvuru Takip Paneli)
- Visual Kanban or tabular swimlanes utilizing neutral tinted containers:
  - `Başvurulacak` (Slate `#F1F5F9`)
  - `Başvuruldu` (Turquoise `#F0FDFA`)
  - `Yanıt Bekleniyor` (Amber `#FFFBEB`)
  - `Görüşme / Kabul` (Emerald `#ECFDF5`)
- Each row highlights the official contact citation and user's saved personalized email draft preview.
